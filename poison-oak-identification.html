Where the deck places it Ubiquitous in the Berkeley hills — Tilden, Strawberry Canyon, every fire trail. The original Bay Area bramble, here long before the giant invasive arrived. A Luther Burbank introduction gone rampant; it blankets every East Bay creek and empty lot.
