Who it is for

Use it when you are trying to recognize the plants you keep seeing on Berkeley walks, campus edges, street trees, gardens, and nearby East Bay trails.

The app is built for practice, not expert verification. It helps you learn names and visual patterns so the real plants feel less anonymous.

How the quiz works

You look at field photos and guess the plant. The app remembers what you miss, so unfamiliar species, look-alikes, and caution plants come back more often.

The same catalog is available in Browse plants mode when you want to search by name instead of quiz yourself.

What the catalog covers

The catalog includes forageable plants, common trees, garden escapes, and plants that are useful to recognize because they should not be eaten.

Every species is labeled with the same safety bucket used in the app and the crawlable species directory.

Edible Use with care Recognition only

The safety boundary

Plant identification is risky when it turns into eating advice. Forage Berkeley does not replace a field guide, expert help, local rules, or careful confirmation from multiple sources.

The best use is simple: practice the visual patterns, learn the names, and treat anything edible as a prompt for more careful research.
