What to compare in practice

The scent row is deck context, not a handling instruction. Learn the contrast here, then practice with the species pages and quiz photos.

Field mark Poison Hemlock Wild Fennel
Deck label Recognition only. Do not eat. Edible in the deck, but not eating permission.
Stems Smooth hairless stems with purple or maroon blotches. Solid green stems.
Leaves Fern-like leaves. Feathery thread-like leaves.
Flowers White flower umbels. Yellow flower umbels.
Scent note The deck notes a musty or mousy smell and no anise scent. The deck notes a strong anise or licorice smell when crushed. Do not handle plants to test this.
Deck story The plant that killed Socrates, and it's common in damp, disturbed Bay Area ground. A Mediterranean escapee that lines East Bay trailheads and BART tracks.
