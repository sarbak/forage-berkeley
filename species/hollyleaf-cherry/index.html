Hollyleaf Cherry

Prunus ilicifolia

Use with care Native

This page is a recognition practice aid, not an eating guide. Never eat anything based on this page or the app alone. Plants in this group need preparation, restraint, or extra caution. Read the warning before treating them as food.

Shrub plant type
Use with care safety label
Native origin
Season

Cherries late summer–fall

How to ID

Evergreen, stiff glossy holly-like spiny-edged leaves, small white flower spikes, dark red-purple cherries that are nearly all pit.

Watch

Eat only the thin sweet flesh — the seed/pit, leaves and bark are cyanogenic and toxic. Spit the pit.

Uses

Thin pulp eaten fresh; the leached seed was a traditional staple.

A tough native cherry of chaparral slopes, almost all stone and a sliver of sweet.
