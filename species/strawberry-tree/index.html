Strawberry Tree

Arbutus unedo

Edible Introduced

This page is a recognition practice aid, not an eating guide. Never eat anything based on this page or the app alone. Plants in this group have edible uses in the app, but still need confident identification and clean harvesting conditions.

Tree plant type
Edible safety label
Introduced origin
Season

Fruit fall–winter (with flowers)

How to ID

Evergreen relative of madrone, reddish shedding bark, glossy serrated leaves, urn-shaped white flowers AND round bumpy red-orange fruit at once.

Watch

Mealy and bland unless fully ripe and soft; fine in quantity but easy to overdo.

Uses

Ripe fruit fresh or made into jam.

A common ornamental whose bumpy red fruit looks like a lumpy strawberry.
