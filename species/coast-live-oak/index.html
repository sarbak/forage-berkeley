Coast Live Oak

Quercus agrifolia

Use with care Native

This page is a recognition practice aid, not an eating guide. Never eat anything based on this page or the app alone. Plants in this group need preparation, restraint, or extra caution. Read the warning before treating them as food.

Tree plant type
Use with care safety label
Native origin
Season

Acorns drop in fall

How to ID

Evergreen; small cupped holly-like leaves with spiny edges; gray bark; broad rounded crown.

Watch

Acorns are bitter with tannins and must be ground and leached before eating — never raw in quantity.

Uses

Leached acorn flour for mush and bread.

The defining tree of the East Bay and a staple food of the Ohlone for millennia.
